## Exam 24.08.2021

A survey examined students' opinions on holding exams in person. The probability that a student responds positively is $0.5$. Determine the probability that at least $15$ of $90$ surveyed students respond positively.

## Exam June 2021

Jana takes her child to kindergarten twice a week, but she often picks the child up a few minutes late. The kindergarten requires parents to arrive on time and charges $2$ dollars for each minute of delay.

Assume that the daily delay in minutes is exponentially distributed with mean $4$ and is independent across days. Determine the probability that over $100$ days Jana pays more than $860$ dollars for late pickup.

## Exam 23.01.2018

Assume $p=\frac{1}{20}$ and that the point errors for the $30$ courses of one student are independent random variables. Determine the probability that the total grading error over the $30$ courses is between $-1.5$ and $1.5$ points.
