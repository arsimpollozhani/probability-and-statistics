\subsection{Problem: Exam (24.08.2021)}
A survey was conducted to examine students' opinions about holding exams with physical presence. The probability that a student answers positively to the survey is $0.5$. Determine the probability that out of $90$ surveyed students, the number of those who answered positively is at least $15$.

\subsection{Problem: Exam (June 2021)}

Jana takes her child to kindergarten twice a week. However, she often picks the child up a few minutes late. The kindergarten has strict rules according to which parents must arrive on time to pick up their child, and they charge $2$ dollars for each minute of delay. Assume that each day the delay time in minutes has an exponential distribution with mean value $4$ and is independent of the delays on other days. Determine the probability that over $100$ days Jana will have to pay more than $860$ dollars for late pickup of her child from kindergarten.


\subsection{Problem: Exam (12.02.2021)}

An intercity bus travels on a route from city A to city B and back to A. For this trip it consumes on average $4$ liters of fuel with a standard deviation of $0.2$. The bus has 196 liters of fuel in its tank. What is the probability that the fuel will not be sufficient for the next 50 trips (round trips)?

\subsection{Problem: (Second Midterm 2019)}

The bulbs of a projector have an average lifetime of $100$ hours with a standard deviation of $75$. A projector at our university is used for 9000 hours during one semester. Determine the probability that $100$ bulbs will be sufficient for one semester.
\textit{Result: {0.90824}}

\subsection{Problem: Exam (01.09.2020)}

The amount of water in a drop has an expected value of $3 ml$ and a standard deviation of $0.8 ml$. The amount of water in one drop is independent of the amount of water in the other drops. What is the probability that in $3$ minutes the glass will overflow?

\subsection{Problem: Exam (07.02.2020)}

\begin{itemize}
  
\item [a)] (10+15) What do you expect to earn in one game with the first and with the second option?
\item [b)] (7+8) For the first option, estimate the probability that after 50 played games you will have more money than the amount you had when you entered the casino.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Problem: Second Midterm 2018}

The probability that a given machine will have some problem during one day is $(p_0 = 0.3)$ (independent of previous problems). \subsection{Problem: Exam (23.01.2018)}

\subsection{Problem: Second Midterm 2015}
Upgrading a software package involves installing $68$ new files. The files are installed sequentially. The installation time is random, and the average installation time for each file is $9$ seconds with a variance of $(2,\text{sec}^2)$.

\begin{itemize}
  \item [a)] What is the probability that $10$ minutes will be sufficient to install the entire package?
\textit{Result: {0.15151}}
\item [b)] A new version of the software has been released. In this version, only $N$ new files need to be installed, and it is known that with probability at least $0.95$, no more than $7$ minutes will be needed to install the entire package. Determine $N$.
\textit{Result: {43}}

\end{itemize}
