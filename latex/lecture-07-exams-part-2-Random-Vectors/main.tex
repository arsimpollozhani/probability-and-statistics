He wins 3 dollars if he draws 2 red cards, 5 dollars if he draws 3 red cards, and 10 dollars if he draws 4 red cards. Let $X$ be the random variable  the gambler's profit, and $Y$ be the random variable the number of blue cards drawn.

\begin{itemize}
\item  [a)]  (12) Determine the probability distribution of the random vector $(X, Y)$.
\item  [b)] (5) (3) Are $X$ and $Y$ independent random variables?.
\item  [c)] (5) What is the probability that the gambler will have a positive profit if he drew more than one blue card?
\item  [d)] (5) What is the expected profit of the gambler?
\end{itemize}
